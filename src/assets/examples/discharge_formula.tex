

$$\frac{\dot m}{A\sqrt{2 \rho \Delta P}}$$


\begin{enumerate}
    \item{$m = $ the mass flow from load cells}
    \item{$\rho = $ the density of the stuff (in this case, carbon dioxide)}
    \item{$\Delta P = $ is the difference between downstream PT and ambient pressure
            $$\Delta P = (P_{\text{downstream}} - P_{\text{ambient pressure}})$$

            ambient pressure is local data from the weather station}
